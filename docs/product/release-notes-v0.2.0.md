# runwai 0.2.0

- Fixes Codex refresh when the 5-hour limit is absent.
- A clearer daily budget and weekly overview, sized to fit the popup.
- A larger Activity chart with Today / Full window ranges and hover readouts.
- More accurate history around midnight and idle periods.
- Now focused on Codex. Spark and Gemini are no longer shown or refreshed;
  their previously saved local data is left untouched.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Unzip and drag `runwai.app` into Applications,
replacing the old app after quitting it.
