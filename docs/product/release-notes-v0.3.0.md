# runwai 0.3.0

Your usage, your pace, and what your agent is up to.

- Chart-first Usage with Day, Full window, and Historical views.
- Burn rate front and center, with clearer handling of breaks and quota resets.
- New Activity view powered by bundled Lowdown: recent projects, session switching,
  and short progress summaries.
- Expand updates to read the full Markdown original or final answer.
- More reliable loading, error recovery, and retries, with a tighter native UI.

Summaries use your Codex login and allowance. Turn them off in Settings to keep
using originals and cached summaries. No separate Lowdown installation is needed.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Signed and notarized for Apple Silicon and Intel.
Quit runwai, unzip `runwai-macos.zip`, and drag `runwai.app` into Applications,
replacing the previous copy. Your local history and preferences stay intact.
